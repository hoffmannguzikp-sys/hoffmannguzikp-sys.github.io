Privacy Policy – Mine Rush Party

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Mine Rush Party ("the game") processes and why. The game is published under the brand One-Shot Apps.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Patryk Hoffmann-Guzik, Poland, trading as One-Shot Apps.
Contact: OneShotAppForMobileDevices@gmail.com

2. Data stored only on your device

Your player name, the game settings (sound, vibration, lobby options), the last address you joined and whether you bought "No ads" are saved only in the game's private storage on your phone. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

The game has no user accounts and does not ask for your email, contacts, photos or location.

3. Playing over local Wi-Fi

Multiplayer games run directly between the phones on the same local network; there are no game servers. When you host or join a game, your player name and your moves are sent only to the other players' devices on that network. The hosting phone announces the game on the local network so that others can find it. Nothing of this leaves the local network or reaches us.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows a banner ad at the top of the screen through Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • information about ad interactions, crash and performance diagnostics.

Google processes this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can refuse; you will then see non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy settings button in the game's main menu.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads).

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalized ads and our legitimate interest in funding a free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for non-personalized ads and fraud prevention.

6. The "No ads" purchase

You can remove the ads with a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we do not receive your payment details. The game only remembers on your device that ads were removed, and asks Google Play to restore the purchase after a reinstall.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict processing of your data, to object, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold data that identifies you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google; we will help if you contact us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, uodo.gov.pl), or the authority in your own country.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address, with the date of the last update above.
