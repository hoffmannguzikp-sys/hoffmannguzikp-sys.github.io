Privacy Policy – Heuristic: Yes/No & Picker

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what data the Android app Heuristic: Yes/No & Picker ("the app") processes and why. The app is published under the brand One-Shot Apps.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Patryk Hoffmann-Guzik, Poland, trading as One-Shot Apps.
Contact: OneShotAppForMobileDevices@gmail.com

2. Data stored only on your device

Your option lists, list names, weights and app settings (such as theme, vibration and visual effects) are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Questions you type on the Yes/No screen are not saved at all. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC: a banner at the top of the screen and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to support us. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • information about ad interactions, crash and performance diagnostics.

Google processes this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can refuse; you will then see non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings › Privacy settings.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads).

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalized ads and our legitimate interest in funding a free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for non-personalized ads and fraud prevention.

5. Other Google Play features

After you have used the app for a while, it may show Google Play's in-app review dialog once. This is handled by Google Play; we do not receive your identity, only the public rating and review if you choose to post one.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict processing of your data, to object, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold data that identifies you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google; we will help if you contact us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, uodo.gov.pl), or the authority in your own country.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address, with the date of the last update above.
